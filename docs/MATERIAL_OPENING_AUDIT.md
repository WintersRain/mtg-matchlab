# Experimental material stress audit

`python3 material_opening_audit.py examples/material-basic.json --samples 100 --seed 42 --output /tmp/material-result.json`

Standalone local tool, not a `matchlab` subcommand or full-game simulator. Reuses `opening_audit.payments` and `parse_cost`. An explicit 60-slot card model declares land colors/types, entry model; or body cost, legendary flag, fixed ETB token count, optional offspring (additional two mana), and anchor flag. Public example is an intentionally abstract mechanism fixture, not a recommended legal deck.

Supported lands: basic/untapped/shock/fast/tapped/passage. Passage chooses ONE basic actually remaining in the physical library, consumes it, replaces the sacrificed Passage, shuffles the remaining library including London bottoms, and untaps the fetched land if controlling four after replacement. No rainbow substitution. Pilot fetches immediately and cannot strategically hold an uncracked Passage; this is a policy limitation. Shock costs accumulate. No Arena hand smoothing.

London down to five: retain 2–4 lands and an actual <=turn-two body using only offered hand, with known-hand simulation for bottoming. Forced five recorded. Turns use visible cards, never future library order; same initial physical-slot permutations across candidates/mulligan depths. Fetches change subsequent library streams. This pairing is not a paired full-game estimate. A bounded greedy land heuristic values current body deployment then known-hand next-turn possibilities; its lookahead is a heuristic (not proof the speculative next-turn extra land is untapped). Actual casts always honor tappedness and payments.

Primary score chooses bodies produced, then mana used. This can prioritize a token maker over an anchor; availability and actual cast metrics differ. Two abstract opponent interventions remove one creature after turn 2 and turn 3 (nontoken/high-cost first), NOT targeting or resolving a specific opposing card. Token ETBs and offspring are modeled. Combat, stats, counters, targeted abilities, Ant-Man token activations, Commander counter triggers, card-draw triggers, spells, recursion, protection, opposing removal costs and wins ARE NOT. Use legal hand-worked opposing-card fixtures separately; do not use this model to compare decks' full resource/removal performance. `jenova_with_target` means another raw body remained when an anchor was cast; not that combat/trigger resolved against real interaction.

Reports retain offered seven/bottom/accepted hand, actual draws, lands, fetch choice, costs, cast/offspring and removals for the first 12 trials and additional successful anchor examples. Means ending `_bodies...` are body counts, NOT success probabilities. Forced-five failures and retained-size early-body counts have explicit denominators. All 200/1000-sample numbers are policy stress estimates, never user mulligan forecasts.

Run regression tests: `python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -p test_material_opening_audit.py -v`. They cover third/fourth fetch timing, exhausted targets, basic-only restriction, entry tappedness, harmless turn-one fetch preceding a GU cast and a two-removal surviving-target fixture.
