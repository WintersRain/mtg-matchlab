## Direction and cost boundary

Proposed ordinary deployment ceiling: four mana, emphasizing meaningful early attackers and immediate three/four-mana impact. This is a working direction, not a validated curve. Elspeth, Storm Slayer and Smile at Death are competing five-mana payoff/recovery candidates; Smile's creature package is evaluated through actual upkeep-to-combat consequences, not automatically assigned to a different archetype. Vibrance's cheap evoke mode is interaction/fixing, not an early surviving body. Miles is a two-mana early play with a six-mana late-game option.

Important correction: Miles Morales // Ultimate Spider-Man is modal_dfc. Ultimate can be cast directly for six; transforming an existing Miles also costs six. Directly casting Ultimate does not produce Miles's front-face entry counters. His attack trigger doubles counters only on Spiders and legendary creatures, not every creature or a noncreature legendary planeswalker.

## User's pictured seeds

- Lightning, Army of One: high-priority aggressive legend; first strike, trample and lifelink make counters immediately valuable. Player combat damage enables Stagger; simultaneous first-strike damage is not retroactively doubled.
- Ouroboroid: high-priority four-mana board payoff. Beginning-of-combat trigger can buff established attackers the turn it enters; no haste required. Nonlegendary/non-Spider, so Ultimate does not double its counters. Pre-combat power increases amplify its grant.
- Tyvar, the Pummeler: resilient three-mana legend, costly later team-pump sink. Indestructibility requires another untapped creature and taps Tyvar; not free protection and not trample.
- Elspeth, Storm Slayer: conditional five-mana exception; zero ability combines counters with flying immediately. Not a creature, so Ultimate does not double her loyalty.
- Smile at Death: active aggressive recovery/payoff candidate, not deferred merely for being delayed. Opposing removal naturally supplies targets; selecting independently useful low-power creatures can restore pressure and amplify existing attackers. Initial five-mana deployment and survival until upkeep remain costs. Printed graveyard power, not former battlefield counters, determines ordinary eligibility; Miles and Ouroboroid qualify.
- Vibrance: utility/flex candidate, not primary aggressive body. Evoke RR gives damage, GG gives land-to-hand plus life; RG satisfies neither condition. Five-mana casting can pay both RR and GG using generic portions and receive both triggers. Search is not land-to-battlefield ramp.

## Expanded creature pass

[Green/colorless records](../research/naya-miles/expanded-g.json) and [white/red/multicolor records](../research/naya-miles/expanded-wr.json) preserve exact text, released Arena-printing evidence, and per-card strengths/limitations. Halana and Alena appears in both role partitions; these are not two deck slots.

Priority additions: Jennifer Walters (cheap legendary spell restriction), Michelangelo (counter replacement), Halana and Alena (counter-scaled haste), Raph & Leo (extra combat), Hardened Academic (hasty evasion and optional discard), Hexing Squelcher (spell/creature disruption protection), Agrus Kos (double strike and blocker suppression), and Warden of the Grove (body/counter production). Spectacular Spider-Man is an independent early protector/attacker that does not qualify for Smile. Other alternatives remain in the source records, not discarded.

Worked package: Smile returns Halana and Alena plus Ouroboroid, adding a counter to each. At beginning of combat, resolve Halana and Alena first targeting Ouroboroid: their power 3 grants it three counters and haste. Ouroboroid now has power 5 and grants five counters to the team, becoming a hasty power-10 attacker. Neither Halana and Alena nor other newly returned creatures automatically gain haste. No trample or guaranteed lethal is claimed. This is rules/arithmetic validation, not a played game.

Michelangelo returned by Smile is on the battlefield when Smile then puts counters on the returned creatures; his replacement effect increases each grant. Raph & Leo's extra combat also repeats beginning-of-combat abilities (Ouroboroid and Halana and Alena), but only their selected attacking creatures untap. Jennifer's spell restriction does not disable abilities. No quantities or constructed list yet.
